# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two audiences at once. The primary audience is people evaluating the maker's craft: recruiters, hiring managers, and peers who open the app for two minutes and judge taste, polish, and attention to detail. The secondary audience is casual players on any device who just want a quick, good-looking trivia round. Both meet the same surface, so it has to read as a finished, considered product on first glance and still be genuinely fun to play through ten questions.

## Product Purpose

A single-screen trivia game: pick a category, answer ten AI-generated questions one at a time, watch a constellation of stars light up as you go, and land on a scored finale. It exists to be a portfolio showpiece that demonstrates design and front-end craft through a small, complete, delightful interaction, not through feature breadth. Success is someone finishing a round, feeling the atmosphere, and remembering the constellation-as-progress idea.

## Brand Personality

Serene, cosmic, premium. The voice is quiet and evocative ("A star ignites", "Charting the sky"), never loud or gamified. Calm confidence over hype. The interface should feel like a still night sky: unhurried, atmospheric, and precise, rewarding attention rather than demanding it.

## Anti-references

- **Generic quiz-app SaaS** (Kahoot, Quizizz): primary-color cartoon energy, bubbly rounded everything, mascot cheer.
- **Gamified neon casino**: slot-machine glitz, coin showers, aggressive reward loops, streak-pressure dopamine.
- **Flat corporate dashboard**: Material or Bootstrap card grids, gray-on-white, zero atmosphere.
- **AI-generated slop**: gradient-text headers, glassmorphism everywhere, hero-metric templates, endless identical card grids.

## Design Principles

- **Progress is the artwork.** The score is not a number in a corner; it is the constellation drawing itself. State and beauty are the same object.
- **One working accent.** Teal does all the interactive lifting. Gold is earned (correct answers, the finale). Color means something; it is never decoration.
- **Atmosphere, then interface.** The night sky sets mood first; the UI sits lightly on top in restrained glass, never fighting the backdrop.
- **Quiet motion with intent.** Every animation maps to a meaning (a star igniting, the sky charting). No motion for motion's sake, no bounce.
- **Complete over broad.** A small flow executed impeccably beats a large one done adequately. Every phase gets finished-product care.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Keyboard-operable throughout (Tab between answers, Enter to select, visible focus rings). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (starfield, aurora, and pulses settle to static). Watch contrast of dim "fog" text on the near-black canvas and of the teal accent on glass surfaces. Progress conveyed by the constellation must also be available to screen readers via a live text fallback, never by color or shape alone.
